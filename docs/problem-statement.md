# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Beginner and intermidiate Silat Cekak practitioner.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Solo practise without expert instructor make Silat Cekak practitioner feels difficult to determine whether their pose is correct or not.  

## Why does it matter?
Silat practititoner may apply bad habit in silat cekak technique. 

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Beginner and intermediate Silat Cekak practitioner need to practise their technique so that it be easier if they go to next level. practise independently makes it tough to know if the technique is executing properly without a qualified instructor watching over. Minor errors in basic body positioning like misaligned joints can easily turn into bad habits and ultimately ruin the effectiveness of the training(Watanabe et al., 2026).
